# Voice & Tone Guidelines

Our communication should reflect a tool that gets out of the way to let teams do their best work.

## Brand Persona: The Efficient Collaborator

- **Professional:** We value precision and reliability.
- **Direct:** We don't waste time with fluff. We say what is necessary.
- **Approachable:** We use a friendly, helpful tone without being overly casual.
- **Empowered:** We speak to users as experts in their fields.

## Grammar & Mechanics

- **Title Case:** Use for Page Titles and Main Buttons (e.g., "Create New Project").
- **Sentence Case:** Use for Subtitles, Helper Text, and Labels (e.g., "Set the due date for this task").
- **Efficient Copy:** If you can say it in 5 words, don't use 10.

## Error Message Guidelines

- **Non-Blaming:** Don't say "You entered the wrong date." Say "Please enter a valid date."
- **Actionable:** Always tell the user how to resolve the issue.
- **Brief:** Keep instructions to a single sentence when possible.
